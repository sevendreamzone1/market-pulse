CE / PE market analysis

Strategy-based CE / PE recommendations with visible agreement, conflicts, and risk levels.

STRATEGY CONFLUENCE
CE = call; PE = put. Index levels and option premiums use different units. Premium scenarios require option-chain quotes. These rules are unvalidated; options can lose the entire premium.

How recommendations are built

The catalog evaluates market structure, price action, candlestick patterns, trend, momentum, volume, intraday, volatility, liquidity concepts, and options context. Expand each category to inspect its exact rule, values, and result. Confirmed pivots use two bars on each side; liquidity labels are price-pattern heuristics, not evidence of institutional orders.

Each directional category receives equal weight. A recommendation needs at least five available categories, at least four in one direction, at least 60% category agreement, and a lead of three categories over the opposite direction. Missing inputs are excluded and conflicting signals can produce NO TRADE. Agreement is not a probability of profit. Options Greeks and positioning are descriptive risk context, not directional votes.

Buy consideration requires a new completed five-minute close beyond the displayed 20-bar high/low trigger while confluence persists. The underlying stop uses the tighter of the last five closes and 1.5 ATR14. Targets are 1R and 2R. Premium stops use actual option-candle ATR14 and the five-bar swing low; no premium history means no premium targets. Recalculate using your broker's actual fill.

Exit on the stop, underlying invalidation, or confluence loss. Consider partial T1 and remainder T2. Time exit: 30 minutes from entry or 15:15 IST. New entries are withheld outside 09:30–14:45 IST, when underlying candles are over ten minutes old, or when option quotes fail freshness, spread, or depth checks.

Strategy categories use scheduled five-minute snapshots. Connect Live API quotes to confirm completed live candle breakouts and monitor option bid-based stops and targets in the shared panel above. Keep that tab open; continue managing orders and risk in your broker. No fills are tracked or orders placed. These strategy rules have not been backtested; options can lose the full premium.

NO BLACK BOX

Know what’s behind the signal.

EMA 9 / 21 measures short-term trend. Wilder RSI 14 measures momentum. A bullish setup requires EMA 9 above EMA 21 and RSI between 50 and 70. A bearish setup requires EMA 9 below EMA 21 and RSI between 30 and 50. Other combinations are neutral. These are observations, not validated predictions or trade instructions.

Freshness comes first

Signals are withheld when the last completed candle ended more than 20 minutes ago. Scheduled snapshots target five-minute updates during weekday market hours; provider delays and GitHub Actions queues can extend this. Market session times exclude holiday checks.

Snapshots use Yahoo Finance or the configured Upstox API. Each signal identifies its feed. TradingView controls its own chart feed and exchange delays. Snapshots represent completed candles; they are not tick-by-tick live quotes.
