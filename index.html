Know what’s behind the signal.

EMA 9 / 21 measures short-term trend. Wilder RSI 14 measures momentum. A bullish setup requires EMA 9 above EMA 21 and RSI between 50 and 70. A bearish setup requires EMA 9 below EMA 21 and RSI between 30 and 50. Other combinations are neutral. These are observations, not validated predictions or trade instructions.

Freshness comes first

Signals are withheld when the last completed candle ended more than 20 minutes ago. Scheduled snapshots target five-minute updates during weekday market hours; provider delays and GitHub Actions queues can extend this. Market session times exclude holiday checks.

Yahoo Finance via yfinance supplies indicative snapshots for personal research. TradingView controls its own chart feed and exchange delays. Guaranteed real-time data needs an authorized data provider.
