CE / PE market analysis

One five-minute trend-breakout method with visible checks, confirmation, and risk levels.

TREND BREAKOUT
CE = call; PE = put. Index levels and option premiums use different units. Premium scenarios require option-chain quotes. These rules are unvalidated; options can lose the entire premium.

How CE / PE scenarios are built

One decision method: five-minute trend breakout. CE needs EMA9 above EMA21, a rising EMA21, and the latest close above EMA9. PE needs the opposite. Mixed checks mean NO TRADE. The old catalog is optional diagnostics and does not vote on entries.

Require at least 60 valid completed in-session candles and 20 consecutive candles from today's 09:15 IST open. Earliest watch is 10:55 IST. The CE trigger is today's latest 20-bar high plus 0.1 ATR14; PE uses the 20-bar low minus 0.1 ATR14. A later fully observed five-minute live candle must close beyond the armed trigger. The live panel fixes that trigger while waiting; watches expire after ten minutes from their source candle or on missed/gapped confirmation.

Underlying stops use the tighter of the five-bar low/high and 1.5 ATR14. Index levels are points. Premium levels use the actual option ask, option ATR14 and five-bar swing low; targets are 1R/2R. Contract expiry, spread, depth, freshness and no-chase checks must pass. No valid option candles means no premium targets.

Exit on premium SL/T2, index invalidation, or a fresh neutral/opposite method decision. T1 suggests a partial exit. Time exit: 30 minutes after entry or 15:15 IST, whichever comes first. New entries require the 09:30-14:45 IST weekday window, fresh analysis and live depth; the warm-up further delays readiness. Holidays are not checked.

Indicators refresh from scheduled five-minute snapshots, not on every tick. Live quotes confirm breakouts and observe exits; keep the live tab open. Refreshing the page does not fetch new provider candles. These are unvalidated research rules, not orders, actual fills, or guaranteed returns.

NO BLACK BOX

Know what’s behind the signal.

EMA 9 / 21 measures short-term trend. Wilder RSI 14 measures momentum. A bullish setup requires EMA 9 above EMA 21 and RSI between 50 and 70. A bearish setup requires EMA 9 below EMA 21 and RSI between 30 and 50. Other combinations are neutral. These are observations, not validated predictions or trade instructions.

Freshness comes first

Signals are withheld when the last completed candle ended more than 20 minutes ago. Scheduled snapshots target five-minute updates during weekday market hours; provider delays and GitHub Actions queues can extend this. Market session times exclude holiday checks.

Snapshots use Yahoo Finance or the configured Upstox API. Each signal identifies its feed. TradingView controls its own chart feed and exchange delays. Snapshots represent completed candles; they are not tick-by-tick live quotes.
